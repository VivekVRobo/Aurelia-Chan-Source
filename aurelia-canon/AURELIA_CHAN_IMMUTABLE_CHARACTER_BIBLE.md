# Aurelia-chan — Immutable Character Bible

**Status:** FINAL / LOCKED — v1.0  
**Character:** Aurelia-chan (オーレリアちゃん)  
**Role:** Executive Career Mentor & Life Strategist  
**Age:** 33 — permanently fixed  
**Canonical height:** 170 cm / 5'7" — permanently fixed  
**Primary visual authority:** the 15 master sheets in `master-sheets/`

## Canon authority and change control

This file is the permanent project source of truth for Aurelia-chan. All future illustration, animation, UI art, 3D work, expression portraits, and marketing assets must follow it.

Priority when sources disagree:

1. The copied visual master sheets in `master-sheets/`
2. This immutable character bible
3. Earlier prompts, drafts, and generated assets

Do not change an immutable feature unless the owner explicitly approves a new version of this document (for example, `v1.1`). Earlier generated expression images are **pre-final reference drafts** and are not allowed to override this canon.

## Core identity

- Mature, calm, highly observant, precise, disciplined, and emotionally composed.
- A trusted professional mentor: authoritative without being hostile; warm without being childish.
- She never appears impulsive, cartoonishly excited, cruel, flirtatious, or exaggerated.
- Her defining visual impression is refined executive confidence with quiet intelligence.

## Immutable face and head

- Elegant oval face with a soft, refined jawline and balanced adult proportions.
- Straight, small refined nose; natural rose lips; medium-thickness defined eyebrows with a gentle arch.
- Sharp, almond-shaped eyes with a calm, direct, intelligent gaze.
- Makeup remains natural and restrained: no glitter, heavy blush, or dramatic color shifts.
- Never alter apparent age, face shape, ethnicity, or adult maturity.

## Immutable eye canon

- **Shape:** slightly almond, elongated horizontally; upper lid coverage about 25–30%, lower lid about 15–20%.
- **Outer limbal ring:** deep navy/charcoal `#0E1624`.
- **Mid iris:** sapphire blue `#1E3A8A`.
- **Main iris:** sapphire blue `#2F5FB3`.
- **Inner ring:** royal violet `#5A3FA0`.
- **Pupil:** centered deep black.
- **Highlight:** cool, natural top-left highlight `#E6F1FF`.
- The eyes must never become green, turquoise, purple-only, gray-only, or brown.

## Immutable hair canon

- **Color:** polished jet black / espresso black; base approximately `#0E0E12`.
- **Cut:** compact, layered short bob; chin length at the front and nape length at the back.
- **Part:** subtle off-center part to Aurelia's left.
- **Silhouette:** crown has soft controlled volume; side is lightly tucked behind an ear; ends curve subtly inward.
- **Texture:** smooth, fine, silky, healthy natural shine with restrained highlights.
- Never use long hair, ponytails, buns, fringe-heavy cuts, fantasy colors, frizz, split ends, or a messy silhouette.

## Immutable skin canon

- **Canon name:** warm ivory.
- **Undertone:** neutral-warm; smooth, luminous, refined, healthy, and low-redness.
- **Base:** `#F6EDE6`; shadow `#EBDCCA`; mid tone `#F1E3D6`; warmth `#F6DCC8`; highlight `#FFF6F0`.
- Lighting may alter values only; it must never change her underlying skin tone.
- Never render her tan, bronze, yellow, pink/rosy, gray, heavily textured, or pore-emphasized.

## Immutable body canon

- Lean, toned, balanced, athletic-feminine build with professional, realistic proportions.
- **Height:** 170 cm / 5'7".
- **Proportion system:** exactly 7.5 heads tall.
- **Measurements:** head 20.6 cm; neck 9.0 cm; shoulder width 38.0 cm; bust 87.0 cm; waist 63.0 cm; hips 92.0 cm; arm length (shoulder to wrist) 57.0 cm; leg length (hip to floor) 101.0 cm; inseam 79.0 cm; foot length 24.5 cm.
- Upright, natural posture; level relaxed shoulders; neutral spine; soft knees; weight evenly balanced.
- Hips are the widest point; waist is narrow but never exaggerated; limbs remain long, defined, and realistic.
- Use the front, side, back, master-body, full-body, and height blueprint sheets for any full-body or pose asset.

## Immutable wardrobe and accessories

### Core executive outfit

- Jet-black/very dark navy tailored single-breasted blazer; notched lapels, structured shoulders, tapered waist, one-button closure, single back vent.
- Ivory-white soft silk blouse with clean collar and naturally rolled sleeves.
- High-waisted charcoal/black slim-straight trousers with pressed crease and side pockets.
- Black leather belt with a restrained antique-gold rectangular buckle.
- Pointed-toe black patent-leather heels, approximately 8.5 cm stiletto heel.

### Canonical minimal accessories

- Small gold stud earrings.
- Slim gold pendant necklace.
- Black watch with gold face/accents.
- No wedding ring, excessive jewelry, bold patterns, bright bags, or loud fashion details.

**Resolved visual-canon conflict:** the master sheets visibly and repeatedly establish gold studs, a slim gold pendant, black-and-gold watch, and a gold belt buckle. Those visual references override the earlier text-only silver/no-necklace draft.

## Rendering and palette

- Premium semi-realistic anime/editorial character art: precise linework, refined fabric folds, polished yet natural soft cel shading.
- Never shift into chibi, childish anime, photorealism, low-detail cartoon art, fantasy fashion, or sexualized presentation.
- Primary clothing palette: jet black `#0E0E12`, charcoal `#1D1D22`, ivory white `#F7F7F7`, slate gray from the visual master samples, and antique gold `#C9A227`.
- Eye accents may use deep sapphire and royal violet; do not introduce unapproved saturated colors.

## Expression canon

All expressions remain mature, measured, and subtle. Approved baseline expressions are:

- Neutral / observing
- Serious
- Disappointed (one eyebrow may rise slightly)
- Analyzing
- Thinking
- Slight smile
- Strict / warning
- Confident
- Encouraging
- Rare soft smile

Even strong emotions must be controlled: no wide shouting mouth, manic laughter, exaggerated crying, rage, slapstick surprise, or childish pouting.
